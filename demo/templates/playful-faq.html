Everything you wanted to know about Actual CSS.

What makes Actual CSS different?

A framework that embraces the browser instead of replacing it. Native elements, semantic classes, and a little room for personality.

Can I create custom animations?

Absolutely. Keep the component's structure and bring your own motion. Actual handles the foundations; your application decides how they feel.

Do I need JavaScript?

Not for this one. Modern CSS can animate the opening and closing of native details. Other browsers still get an accessible disclosure without animation.

Can I make it look completely different?

Yes. Change the palette, radius, icon or timing without adding a new framework variant. The behavior remains the browser's responsibility.

This page keeps Actual's .accordion.separated, native <details name> grouping, and documented marker hooks. The dark palette, pill marker, hover lift, and content transition are local CSS, not framework defaults.

The interaction works without JavaScript. Browsers that do not support animated ::details-content still get a fully functional accordion; reduced-motion preferences disable the effects.
