    Change the palette without touching a component.

Previous and next are ::scroll-button(); the dots are ::scroll-marker. Both are progressive: without them, the rail remains a native horizontal scroller. For mouse drag, looping, autoplay or identical controls in every browser, reach for a dedicated carousel library such as Swiper.
