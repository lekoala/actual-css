Before you print

How long does a run take?

Most runs leave the studio within five working days of proof approval. Fluorescent inks add a day to dry.

Which files do you accept?

A PDF with one greyscale page per ink layer. We send a template with bleed and safe margins.

Can I print on coloured paper?

Yes: twelve recycled stocks, from bone white to kraft. Ask for the swatch book.

What if the colours drift?

Riso is never pixel-exact and layers shift by a millimetre. If a proof misses your intent, the second one is free.
