Pele × Actual CSS

A small <pele-diagram> web component hands Mermaid text to pele, loaded from CDN, which draws SVG themed with Actual CSS tokens. Switch themes and watch the diagrams follow without a re-render.

Event history — HTML, no library

A list of what happened, with links and actions in it, is content: .timeline keeps it in the DOM, selectable and accessible without JavaScript.

  1. Today, 09:30 Ada Morgan confirmed the pilot schedule.
  2. October 16 Booking flow merged.
  3. October 14 Patient portal estimate raised by a week.

Chronology — a timeline diagram

Periods and milestones laid out graphically: a drawing, not a list to interact with. Sections take a palette turned from --primary.

Planning — a Gantt chart

Critical tasks take --danger through --pele-critical; the rest follows the surface tokens.

Process — a flowchart

Nodes fill with --surface-subtle, edges take --text-muted, corners follow --radius.

Which one?

  • Events a reader scans, follows links from or acts on: .timeline.
  • A chronology, a schedule or a process to look at: a Pele diagram on the token bridge.
  • Progress through the steps of a flow: .steps.
