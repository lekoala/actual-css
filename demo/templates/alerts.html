Alert icon alignment

The leading icon is centred on the first line box, then lowered onto the x-height by half the gap between the font's cap height and x-height. That offset is derived from --alert-font-size, --alert-line-height, --alert-icon-size and the font itself, so each stage below changes one hook and every icon should stay level.

Icon sources

A webfont glyph and an inline SVG in a 24 or a 16 viewBox all fill the same --alert-icon-size box, so they share one centre. The SVG carries its own stroke or fill; the webfont glyph takes the alert's ink.

Icon sets

The same warning from each set, in the same box. The box is centred; where the ink sits inside it is the set's drawing, so an outline triangle can read high and a filled one grounded. A set-specific correction belongs to the application, through --alert-icon-nudge: the second case lowers the Tabler triangle by 1px.

Why Tabler reads high: on its 24 grid the triangle's ink runs from 1.67 to 21, so 1.67 of margin on top and 3 below — its centre sits 2.8% above the box centre, and the ink fills only 80% of the box. Bootstrap Icons' triangle runs from 1 to 15 on a 16 grid: centred, filling 87.5%. Both effects lift Tabler's base, the edge the eye compares with the baseline, by about 1px; a thin outline apex lifts it further.

Actions and lists

A trailing button is content: a .cluster pushes it to the end and centres it on the text block, while the icon stays on the first line. A list is a plain sibling of the title and keeps its bullets. .close takes the first line whatever follows the title.
