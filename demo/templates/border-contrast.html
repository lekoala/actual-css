Control borders

Explore the balance between subtlety and visibility.

--border-control is the resting edge of a field, a checkbox, a radio, an OTP cell, a choice card, a join addon, or a neutral outline button. It is derived from the ink: --border-control-mix sets how much of --text goes in, and the rest is the opaque surface.

The same form is rendered at 25, 35 and 45 % of ink, in both schemes, on the page and on --surface-subtle. Every ratio below is measured from this page's computed styles — the color-mix(in oklab, …) result included — never copied.

Focus bench · Contrast contexts

Ink contribution

  • edge · fill — the border against the control's own fill.
  • edge · surface — the border against the panel behind the control.
  • fill · surface — the fill itself, the cue a softer edge would lean on.
  • WCAG 1.4.11 asks 3:1 of the visual information that identifies a control, so the two edge rows are the contract and a value under 3:1 is marked *. The fill row is context.

Why 45 %

45 % is the ink share that keeps the default palette at 3:1 against --surface: 3.19 in light, 3.60 in dark. It is set by the control whose border is its only visible boundary — an empty field on a white page, an unchecked checkbox. A full-width field and an 18 px checkbox carry the same ratio, so the small one sets the floor.

A softer edge works when something else identifies the control: a fill that differs from the panel, a visible label, a filled track. The two sides of the edge are reported above because that substitution is what makes a lighter border defensible.

45 % is not a universal guarantee. On --surface-subtle the default palette already measures 2.87 (light) and 3.00 (dark), and a tinted or translucent surface pulls it further. Raise the mix when your surfaces do that; actual-css contrast reports the pair for a real theme.

Where the mix applies

--border-control is declared on :root and on every [data-theme] boundary, so its color-mix() resolves there. Assigning --border-control-mix on an ordinary container changes nothing: the control inherits a colour that was already resolved. Both cards below ask for 35 %; only the island moves.
