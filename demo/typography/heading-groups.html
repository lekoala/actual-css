Heading Groups

Use `hgroup` when a heading has a short subtitle, tagline, or supporting paragraph.

Team

For growing products

<hgroup>
  <h3>Team</h3>
  <p class="muted">For growing products</p>
</hgroup>

hgroup controls its own compact internal spacing. Override the gap with --hgroup-gap; the default is 0.25em. Do not use .stack only to space a heading and subtitle.
